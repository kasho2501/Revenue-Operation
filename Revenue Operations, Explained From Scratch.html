Revenue Operations

Revenue Operations, explained from scratch

In one line: RevOps is how a company makes sure every lead, sale and renewal is handled well, by every team, using the same rules and the same numbers.

A plain-language guide for anyone starting from zero. First learn what RevOps is, then watch it work inside a real business, from the first lead to renewal and expansion.

Part 1

Foundations

Revenue Operations manages the whole path by which a company earns money as one connected system. Marketing, Sales and Customer Success share one process, one data model and one set of numbers, so forecasts are trusted and handoffs keep their context. Without it, each team counts differently, leads wait, deals stall and renewals are found too late.

🎯

New business

Winning customers who have never bought before. Costly, because each one must be found, convinced and onboarded.

🔁

Renewals

Keeping customers when their term ends. It protects revenue already earned, so a leaky renewal process stalls growth.

📈

Upsell & cross-sell

Selling more to existing customers. Usually cheaper than new business because trust already exists.

🧾

Amendments

Mid-term changes such as added seats or co-terming. Small on the surface, but each touches quotes, contracts and billing.

Part 3

Running the Engine

Behind the journey sits a steady operating system. This is the executive view that leadership sees every week.

Operating rhythm

Weekly pipeline review and forecast call, monthly business review, quarterly QBR, annual planning.

Team

RevOps lead, Salesforce admin, analyst, Deal Desk, systems owner and enablement partner.

Territory & quota

Fair territories, realistic quotas and capacity planning matched to market potential.

Data governance

One source of truth, named data owners, quality rules and one metric dictionary.

Part 4

Level Up

Reactive

Siloed teams, manual reporting. Next: agree shared definitions and clean the data.

Organised

Core processes exist. Next: connect the lifecycle across teams and tools.

Integrated

One lifecycle, one data model. Next: automate handoffs and add predictive analytics.

Predictive

Clean data, automation and reliable forecasts. Next: keep improving and govern AI carefully.

Technology stack

Salesforce: CRM, system of recordHubSpot / Marketo: demand and lead dataCPQ: quotes and pricingClari: forecastingGong: conversation insightContracts and billing: booking and invoicesBI: dashboards

AI in RevOps

AI scores leads, summarises pipeline, flags anomalies and drafts follow-ups. It depends on clean data, and people approve decisions on pricing and contracts. Governance decides what AI may do alone and what it may not.
